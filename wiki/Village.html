Village is an area to the south of the Crossroads. It is home to refugees who managed to escape the Town.

All villagers in the Village share the same reputation.

Locations

  • Twins' Land: Heffry and Jeffry, Jack (moves to the Town after the quest "Jack in a Boat")
  • Hangman's House
  • Herm's Store: Herm
  • Village Tavern: Januarius
