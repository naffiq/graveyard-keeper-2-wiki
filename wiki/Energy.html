General Information

Type

Resource

Stack size

100


Energy is one of the resources of the Keeper. Almost all actions consume Energy, with working at a workstation being one of the main uses. Each action has its own Energy cost.

The maximum amount of Energy is 100, but this can be reduced by Insanity. Energy can only be restored by eating food or sleeping. The Energy bar can be viewed at any time in the upper-left corner of the screen, next to the days. The exact current Energy value is not displayed.

Lack of sleep

If the Keeper stays awake for more than two days, he gains the Lack of Sleep effect. While this effect is active, every point of Energy consumed adds 0.5 points of Insanity. The effect is removed by sleeping
