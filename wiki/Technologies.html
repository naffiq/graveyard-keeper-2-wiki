Technologies

Technologies are a game system in Graveyard Keeper 2. The Keeper can unlock new items and workstations in the Tech Tree by spending , , and points, which can be obtained in various ways. Some technologies are hidden or require certain reputation with another character.

Tech Tree

Technologies are divided into several branches, each with its own unlock tree.

  • Building
  • Metallurgy
  • Farming
  • Theology
  • Anatomy and Alchemy
  • Cooking
