Corpse is a game system in Graveyard Keeper 2. They are obtained from Donkey.

Autopsies

A corpse can be placed on an Autopsy Table in the morgue. From there, the corpse's and , body parts, and other parts can be viewed if the corresponding technology has been unlocked.

A corpse left on the ground for to long turns into a pile of ash that needs to be cleaned up by the keeper. Piles of ash produces nothing when cleaned up, The piles of ash no longer count towards the Morgue's corpse totals.
