The hottest job in town. Receive more rewards for cremating corpses.

—speaker


Cremator is a Keeper perk available in the Anatomy talent tree.

Prerequisites

  • Anatomy mastery +1
  • Grave Builder

Effects

Doubles the amount of materials obtained from cremating a corpse.

  • Crematorium I
    • Ash: 10 (previously 5)
    • Salt: 2 (previously 1)
  • Crematorium II
    • Ash: 14 (previously 7)
    • Salt: 4 (previously 2)
