Masteries

Masteries is a game system in Graveyard Keeper 2. Masteries determine which actions the Keeper and zombies can perform. Each action requires a specific type of Mastery. There are five types of Masteries, each represented by a different color and associated with a specific type of action.

  • — associated with corpses.
  • — associated with farming.
  • — associated with construction.
  • — also associated with construction.
  • — associated with books.

A certain amount of Mastery is required to craft items at workstations. The Keeper also needs a specific Mastery level to interact with certain elements of the environment, such as chopping down trees or fishing.

Upgrade mastery

Mastery can be increased by equipping tools or through perks. The Keeper unlocks perks with Inspirations, with no limit on the number of perks that can be unlocked. Zombies, on the other hand, unlock perks by spending , , and points and are limited by the number of they have.
