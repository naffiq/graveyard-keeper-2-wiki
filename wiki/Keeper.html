Keeper

Keeper

Keeper is the character controlled by the player in Graveyard Keeper 2. As the playable character, the Keeper does not have any associated quests or reputation.

The Keeper's appearance can be changed using the Dresser with Mirror in the Home. Most appearances are obtained as quest rewards.
