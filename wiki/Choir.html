General Information

Technology

Location

Church

Choir is a blueprint that can be build in the Church. It can only be built once and has a fixed position to the right of the altar. Once selected, it is built and the blueprint disappears.

The Choir provides +1 , and each zombie assigned to it provides +1 . Zombies can have certain perks in the tree that further increase (the values do not stack; they replace each other):

  • Novice Musician: +2
  • Skilled Musician: +4
  • Maestro: +7

Having a functional Choir is required to progress through the quest "The Nun Also Reads".
