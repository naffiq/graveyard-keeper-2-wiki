Graveyard is a location in the Inquisition Base.

Graveyard Quality

Graveyard Quality can be increased by building some blueprints.

Corpses can be buried in designated locations, and various decorations can then be placed to further improve . However, a grave's quality can never exceed the number of possessed by the corpse buried in it. Currently, the maximum a single corpse can provide is 14
