Zombie Miner

General Information

Technology

Zombie Mining

Location

Old Mine

Zombie Miner is a zombie workstation that can be build in the Old Mine.

Up to two miners can be placed. Each Miner can be assigned one zombie, and each zombie will bring the following every 8 ticks:

  • 1x Iron Ore
  • 2x Coal
  • With the Magpie’s Eye: 10% to get a Gem
