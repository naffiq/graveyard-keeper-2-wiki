Happiness

Happiness is a mechanic. Can be viewed anytime under the energy bar, with the icon . It is directly related to the Church, as it determines the maximum number of attendees at a sermon. The maximum number depends on the Town Quality, which can be viewed at any time in the Town.

Obtaining

  • Town Gratitude gives 5
  • Selling items to traders.

Town Quality

The Keeper can increase the Town Quality by talking to Linda and exchanging Green Scroll, which are obtained by repairing houses in the Town.
