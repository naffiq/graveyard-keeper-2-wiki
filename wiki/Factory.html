Factory is a location under the Inquisition Base.

There are four ways to reach the Factory: from the Writing Basement, from the Old Mine, from beneath the Crossroads, and from the Vineyard Basement. Each route requires clearing at least one Blockage, which requires 4x Iron Detail and 1x Reinforced Beam.

Here, the Keeper can use zombies to automate the production of certain items. Some items can only be produced here. The Factory has storage areas for Log, Stone, Sand, Clay, Coal, Iron Ore, and Marble, which are transported from their respective locations by zombies assigned to the Porter Station.
