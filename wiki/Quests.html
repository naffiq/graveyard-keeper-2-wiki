Quests

Quests is a game system in Graveyard Keeper 2. Quests are the main way the story progresses and can unlock new game systems, locations, and even technologies. Each quest has a main character and a reward, and usually consists of several steps.

Quests can be viewed in the Quest Log, which displays the character associated with the quest, its name, and its description.
